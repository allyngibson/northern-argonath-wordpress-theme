# The Northern Argonath WordPress Theme
In 2012, NPR released a WordPress theme called **Argo**. [Project Argo](http://argoproject.org), the collective title, was a collection of blog tools, a theme and plugins, for member stations to use for their blogs. The idea, according to the project website, was "to build a system that would help a single blogger produce a robust, attractive website day after day."

I was intrigued by both the overall concept, thought the theme itself was attractive and might make a good base for my own site, and felt I might be able to get some use out the plugins. So I began to, in my own way, play.

I had, a few years earlier, abandoned the reverse-posts-blog format, where all of my posts on my site are listed from most recent to oldest and instead adopted more of a magazine approach, where my important posts are featured up front with additional posts that are recent but not featured below. To this day (2026, as I write this), that remains my site's presentation approach.

Argonath, named for the giant statues on the River Anduin in _The Lord of the Rings_, was [developed as a child theme that would implement a magazine-style front end](http://www.allyngibson.com/?p=6561) for an Argo-powered site. I would later rename it The Northern Argonath, after a location from EA Games' cancelled video game, _The Lord of the Rings: The White Council_. Neither name has any connection to what the WordPress theme _did_; it was the Argo/Argonath word similarities and my own innate nerdity that connected them.

These files are presented as-is, and they may not work. There may be something in them that will trigger a fatal error because they call a deprecated function or a plugin that I had installed at the time.

I used The Northern Argonath for about two years, until it was replaced by Lindisfarne in 2014.
